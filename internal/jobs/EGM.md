# jobs

## Overview
Package `jobs` implements EdgeFetch's pure-CPU job & ATS intelligence engine across both `cmd/edgefetch-wasm` (`GOOS=wasip1 GOARCH=wasm`) and native Go builds: compensation and workplace normalization, Schema.org `JobPosting` JSON-LD extraction, EEO/AI legal boilerplate detection, JSON record chunking, 18+ ATS board/posting pre-fetch specs and HTML/JSON parsers, company/domain anti-spoofing filters, redirect filter-loss diagnostics, hardened HTTP `402` (`x402` / `L402`) payment-term sanitization, and ethical `robots.txt` / `Content-Signal` / challenge alternatives.

## Invariants
- **Zero WASM `net/http` / `crypto/tls` Footprint**: No file compiled under `GOOS=wasip1 GOARCH=wasm` imports `net/http`, `crypto/tls`, or `crypto/x509`, keeping `edgefetch-core.wasm` lean for Cloudflare Workers.
- `[pay_workplace.go:ParsePayFromText]`: Caps prose inputs at `65,536` UTF-16 units, enforces digit/decimal lookaround boundaries so version strings (`v1.20`) never swallow adjacent salaries, rejects foreign currency symbols/codes (`₹`, `INR`, `¥`, `JPY`, etc.), ZIP codes, non-monetary counts, and ARR/funding prose, and requires explicit compensation noun phrases plus comma-thousands or hourly formatting on symbol-less ranges.
- `[jsonld.go:InspectRawJobPostingJSONLDLinear]`, `[types.go:SanitizeJobFieldPlain]`: Scans `<script type="application/ld+json">` blocks with quote-aware tag boundaries, preserves `\U000E0069` sentinels when raw, JSON-escaped, or HTML-entity-encoded Unicode Tags (`U+E0000..U+E007F`) appear, and strips invisible runes both before and after entity decoding.
- `[json_chunks.go:ChunkJSONRecords]`, `[json_chunks.go:isSafeUserFilterRegex]`: Fails closed (`0` matches) when a `~`/`~=` `jsonFilter` clause contains malformed regex syntax, backreferences, lookarounds, or quantified groups containing internal quantifiers (e.g. `(a*aaa*)?$`), and caps regex match subjects at `2,048` UTF-16 units.
- `[company_tokens.go:MatchesATSTenantSlugAgainstToken]`, `[matching.go:MatchesAllowedCompanyOrDomain]`: Rejects open job aggregators, shared-hosting suffixes, CentralNic private suffixes, cross-TLD spoofs (`acme.io` vs `acme.com`), hyphenated single-word splits (`st-ripe`), non-Latin homoglyphs (`Не Stripe`, `Stripe Fаke`), and negation/agency tokens while preserving verified multi-word brands (`Hippocratic AI`, `Palo Alto Networks`), `dba` names, and official corporate domains (`about.google`, `metacareers.com`).
- `[x402_blocked.go:ParseX402PaymentTerms]`, `[x402_blocked.go:BuildBlockedInfo]`: Never pays automatically, selects the maximum positive atomic amount across `accepts[]` and header/body sources to defeat cheap decoys, pins verified mainnet USDC to `6` decimals via string-only decimal formatting (never trusting arbitrary `extra.decimals`), allowlists asset/currency tickers, binds `resource` to `https://` on `expectedHost`, and strips Markdown links, URLs, variation selectors, and prompt-injection prose.
